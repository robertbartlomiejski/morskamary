# Boundaries of inference
- Protocol queries, axis_target and source_query are **retrieval intents**, not empirical evidence or validated competences.
- A table of 120 declared queries does not measure educational skills demand in the population.
- 1,080 logical page requests is planned acquisition workload, not 1,080 actual HTTP calls or an estimated sample of publications.
- 54,000 potential pre-dedup rows is not 54,000 unique evidence units.
- Green CI and offline analysis are engineering observations, not proof of live API entitlement, completed acquisition or externally validated semantics.
- H1 can be recalculated only after new evidence and comparable protocol and inputs are established; report effect sizes and biases separately.
- H2 `not_computable` until validated supply and mappings exist; no registry cannot be treated as zero supply.
- H3 requires validated relations, not automatic inference from MARINE/OCEANIC co-presence or missing candidate bridges.
- No new corpus, analysis or empirical H1/H2/H3 result was generated in this audit.
