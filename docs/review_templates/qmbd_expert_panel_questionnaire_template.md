## Instrument purpose

This one-page instrument is designed to standardize future review of QMBD-aligned competence candidates, validation decisions, vocabulary dictionaries, and evidence-to-axis assignments.

## Rating scale

Use the following 1-7 scale unless otherwise stated:

1. strongly disagree
2. disagree
3. somewhat disagree
4. neither agree nor disagree
5. somewhat agree
6. agree
7. strongly agree
